Our Vision

Not knowing shouldn't
be the default

Where We're Headed

From occasional check-ins
to always current

Most fleet tools were built around periodic check-ins: a location pinged every few minutes, a report generated at the end of the day. We think the default should be continuous instead: a live picture of where every vehicle is and what every rider is doing, updated as it happens rather than reconstructed afterward.

Why Now

Real-time tracking is
finally affordable

Vehicles and phones are more connected than ever, and the infrastructure to process location data continuously, instead of in periodic batches, is now well within reach for a small team to build. That's what makes it possible to offer fleets and individual riders the same standard: always-current tracking, not a snapshot from a few minutes ago.

How We Work With Fleets

What to expect, from
pilot to scale

Working with VEXAR follows a deliberate path: proving value on a small slice of your fleet, then expanding on your timeline.

Step 01

Pilot

We start with a subset of your fleet, confirming the dashboard reflects your vehicles and drivers accurately under your actual operating conditions.

Step 02

Integration

Once validated, VEXAR fits into your existing fleet workflows, an addition to how you already operate, not a replacement for it.

Step 03

Scale

We expand across your full fleet or portfolio, with reporting and history building up as more trips are logged.

Step 04

Ongoing Partnership

We stay engaged after deployment, refining the dashboard and reporting alongside your team as your needs change.
