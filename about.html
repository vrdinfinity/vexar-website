About VEXAR

What is VEXAR

VEXAR is an early-stage technology company built around a simple idea: whoever's responsible for a vehicle should always know what's happening with it.

We're building two products on one shared foundation: a fleet dashboard that gives operators live visibility into their vehicles and drivers, and a personal tracking app that gives individual riders an automatic record of their trips.

Both run on the same real-time location and trip infrastructure, built once and shaped differently for each audience.

The Problem

Fleets run on
stale updates.

Ask most fleet managers where a specific vehicle is right now, and the honest answer is often "I'll have to check." Location updates come from a driver's call, a manual log, or a system that only refreshes every few minutes, so by the time the picture is current, it's already out of date.

Individual riders have it worse: once a trip ends, there's usually no record of it at all.

VEXAR exists to make that information current, automatic, and always available.

Why This Matters

Visibility changes
how you operate.

Fewer surprises

When a vehicle's status is always current, problems like an unplanned stop or an idle vehicle get noticed as they happen, not the next time someone asks.

Less time spent chasing status

Operations leads shouldn't have to call drivers for a location update. A dashboard that's always current frees that time for actual decisions.

A record that just exists

For riders, the value is simpler: trips get logged automatically, so the history is there whenever you want to look back on it, with no extra effort required.
