What We're Building

One engine.
Two ways to stay in the know.

The same real-time location and trip engine runs underneath both a fleet dashboard for operators and a personal tracker for individual riders.

For Fleets

A live view of the
whole operation

Built for the person who has to answer "where's vehicle 12 right now?" A dashboard that keeps operations leads and fleet owners current on every vehicle, driver, and trip without needing to call around for updates.

Live Fleet Map

Every vehicle plotted in real time, color-coded by status, so a scan of the map tells you what's active, idle, or offline.

Vehicle & Driver Registry

A single record for every vehicle and driver: assignment history, trip counts, and current status, always up to date.

Trip History

Every completed trip logged with route, duration, and distance, searchable by vehicle, driver, or date.

Fleet-Wide Reporting

Utilization, activity, and trend reports exportable as PDF, CSV, or Excel, or delivered straight to an inbox.

Operational Alerts

Notice when a vehicle goes offline, drifts off its expected route, or sits idle longer than it should.

A Lightweight Driver View

Drivers get their own simple screen: their trips, their stats, without the operator-side complexity.

For Riders

Every ride,
automatically on record

For the individual rider who wants a running history of their own trips, no manual logging, no separate device. Start riding, and VEXAR keeps the record.

Automatic Trip Tracking

Route, distance, average and top speed captured for every ride, start to finish, without needing to press record.

Personal Ride History

A running log of past rides you can look back on: how far you've gone, and how your pace has changed over time.

Under the Hood

One tracking core,
built once

Both products run on the same underlying location and trip infrastructure, built once, then shaped into two different experiences.

Continuous location tracking: position updates flow in as they happen, not on a delay.

Trip lifecycle management: every trip has a clear start, end, and full route in between.

Role-based access: fleet operators, drivers, and riders each see exactly what's relevant to them.

Built to extend: the same foundation that runs fleet tracking today is designed to carry future capabilities as they're ready.
