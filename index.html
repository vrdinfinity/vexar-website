What is VEXAR

Visibility for the
road, and for the ride

Learn More →

VEXAR is built around one idea: the people responsible for a vehicle should always know what's happening with it. For fleet operators, that means a live dashboard covering every vehicle and driver in the company. For individual riders, it means an automatic record of every trip they take — both running on the same real-time tracking foundation.

The Problem

Most fleets run on
guesswork, not visibility.

Fleet managers are often the last to know when a vehicle goes idle, drifts off route, or simply goes quiet — updates arrive after the fact, if they arrive at all. Riders have it simpler still: once a trip ends, there's usually no record of it anywhere. VEXAR replaces that guesswork with a live, continuous view of every vehicle and every ride.
